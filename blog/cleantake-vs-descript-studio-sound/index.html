CleanTake vs Descript Studio Sound: local workflow or cloud convenience?

By Bronze · October 5, 2026 · 6 min read

The short answer: If you want an all-in-one editor with transcript-based editing and you don't mind uploading your files plus paying a subscription, Descript's Studio Sound (from $16/month) is the polished choice. If all you need is noise removal — no uploads, no subscription, $0 forever — CleanTake does that job locally on your own machine.

No "one is better" verdict here. Different tools for different priorities. Below is everything we could verify, including one thing we couldn't.

What each tool actually is

Descript Studio Sound is one feature inside Descript, a full AI-powered audio/video editor. Its official description: "Instantly improve audio quality of any recording with one click – remove background noise, enhance speech, and get studio-quality sound without a professional setup." Around it sits transcript-based editing, Overdub voice cloning, auto-captions, and stock media — a whole production suite used by 6M+ creators, with teams like Spotify, NYT, and GitHub on the customer logo wall.

CleanTake is a free, open-source tool that does exactly one job: take a noisy recording in, give you the same file back with clean audio. No editor, no transcript, no timeline. It runs 100% on your machine — DeepFilterNet for noise removal, loudness leveled to −16 LUFS — and nothing is ever uploaded anywhere.

Price, spelled out

Descript's pricing (per person, from descript.com/pricing, checked October 4, 2026):

  • Free — $0: 1 media hour/month, 100 one-time AI credits, Studio Sound in "Limited" form, 720p watermarked exports.
  • Hobbyist — $16/mo billed annually ($24/mo monthly): 10 media hours/month, 400 AI credits/month, 1080p watermark-free, Studio Sound included.
  • Creator — $24/mo billed annually ($35/mo monthly): 30 media hours/month, 800 AI credits/month, 4K export, full Studio Sound. Marked "Most Popular."
  • Business — $50/mo billed annually ($65/mo monthly): 40 media hours/month, 1,500 AI credits/month, team features.

So the cheapest way to get full Studio Sound is $16/month paid yearly — $192/year. CleanTake is $0, which over three years is a $576 difference for the noise-removal job alone.

One more pricing nuance worth knowing: Descript meters AI features with credits. The plans include 100–1,500 AI credits, and heavy users report burning through them — check your usage pattern against the caps before committing.

Privacy and upload: the real fork in the road

This is the actual decision point, more than price.

Descript is a cloud product. When you use Studio Sound, your audio goes to Descript's servers for processing. For most creators that's a non-issue — but if you record client calls, unreleased work, medical or legal content, or you simply don't like your raw takes sitting on someone else's infrastructure, it's a real consideration.

CleanTake was built for the opposite trade: your files never leave your disk. There's no account, no upload queue, no retention policy to read — because there's no server involved at all.

Workflow: one job vs. the whole studio

Ask yourself what you're actually trying to do:

  • "I recorded a voiceover/screen recording and the fan hum is ruining it" → that's a one-job problem. CleanTake: drop the mp4 in, get the same mp4 back with clean audio. Done in seconds.
  • "I'm producing a podcast/video series and I live in editing" → that's a suite problem. Descript's transcript editing alone (cut audio by deleting text) is a genuine superpower for long-form work, and Studio Sound rides along inside it.

Buying a full editing suite to remove fan noise is like buying a car to charge your phone. But if you're already driving the car, the phone charger in the dashboard is great.

Who should use which

Use Descript Studio Sound if: you want the full editor anyway (transcript editing, captions, Overdub), you publish often enough to justify $16+/month, and cloud upload doesn't bother you.

Use CleanTake if: you only need noise removal, you want zero uploads, you hate subscriptions, or you process hours of audio and don't want to watch a credit meter.

Use both if: you edit in Descript but pre-clean sensitive takes locally first. Nobody said it has to be a rivalry.

FAQ

Is there a free alternative to Descript Studio Sound?

Yes. CleanTake is free and open-source, runs 100% locally, and handles the core job — removing background noise from voice recordings. Descript's own free plan also includes Studio Sound in "Limited" form (1 media hour/month).

Does Descript Studio Sound upload my audio?

Yes. Descript processes media in its cloud, which is inherent to how Studio Sound works. If your workflow requires zero uploads — client work, unreleased content, or just principle — a local tool like CleanTake is the alternative.

How much does Descript Studio Sound cost?

Full Studio Sound starts at the Hobbyist plan: $16/month billed annually ($24 month-to-month), per descript.com pricing checked October 4, 2026. The free plan includes a limited version.

Can CleanTake replace Descript?

Only for the noise-removal job. Descript is a complete editor — transcript-based editing, auto-captions, voice cloning, stock media. CleanTake deliberately does one thing: noisy file in, clean file out, on your machine, for free.
