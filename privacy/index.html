Privacy

Your audio never leaves your machine. That's not a policy promise — it's how the software is built. Denoising runs locally on your CPU; there is no server to upload to.

What we collect: nothing

  • No audio uploads — processing is 100% local.
  • No telemetry, crash reports, or usage analytics inside the app.
  • No account, no sign-in, no license phone-home.

What the website collects

  • Waitlist emails — the waitlist closed when v1.0.1 shipped (2026-10-02). Any address left earlier gets one launch email at most. Never sold, never shared.
  • Anonymous page events (sample plays, button clicks) — currently logged to the browser console only during testing; when real analytics is connected it will be a privacy-friendly, cookieless provider. No cross-site tracking, ever.

What we haven't audited

Honesty cut both ways: the third-party engine (DeepFilterNet) is open source and we ship it as-is. We have not performed an independent security audit of every dependency. If that matters to you, the open-source engine means you — or anyone — can verify it.
