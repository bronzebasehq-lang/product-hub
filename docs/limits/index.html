Limits

We'd rather lose a download than gain a disappointed user. Here's exactly where CleanTake stops working.

What CleanTake handles

  • Steady environmental noise — fans, air-conditioners, electrical hum, constant room tone.
  • Recorded speech files — podcasts, voiceovers, online lessons, already-recorded interviews.

What CleanTake does NOT handle

  • Overlapping speakers. We tested it: with several voices talking over each other (a busy café), the engine reduces everything evenly — voices included. It cannot isolate one voice from a crowd. This is a different research problem and we don't pretend otherwise.
  • Real-time calls. CleanTake processes files after recording. It is not a live meeting noise-cancellation tool.
  • Music or extreme distortion. The engine is tuned for speech; feeding it music gives unpredictable results.
  • Perfect silence. Noise reduction is a trade-off — push it too far and speech starts to sound watery. We keep the default conservative.
